# RUST-DOC-0008 attribution

Rust and tool documentation is cited for specific framework capabilities.
trybuild, proptest, Loom, Miri, sanitizers, and Criterion remain independent
projects with their own licenses and version behavior.

The evidence hierarchy, claim mapping, fidelity review, flakiness governance,
and operational gates are repository synthesis. A tool's presence does not make
its use mandatory when another evidence source fits the risk better.

This package is not an exhaustive survey of formal verification, mutation
testing, fuzzers, test frameworks, coverage tools, or production experimentation.
