# RUST-DOC-0010 attribution

## Internal source

- Origin: an internal working document proposing a design vocabulary for consuming stage traits
  with bounded associated successor types
- Medium: unpublished internal Markdown
- Use: problem framing, the successor-capability mechanism, and a catalogue of failure modes

The document is not stored in this repository and is not reproduced in it. The doctrine prose,
review gates, decision framework, case study, and executable example are independently written.
The package materially extends the source through boundary analysis, the durable-advancement
limit, the construction-bypass prohibition, the topology-assertion requirement, and the complete
governance and evidence structure. Where the package declines to adopt a claim, the decline and
its reason are recorded in `source-notes.md` rather than left implicit.

Because the originating document coined a term for the mechanism, one attribution obligation
applies to this package specifically: the term is recorded as local vocabulary with its
established family named, and it is not presented as external consensus. `RUST-DOC-0010-R021`
makes that obligation general.

## External sources

Rust language claims cite the Rust Reference and the Rust Book, maintained by the Rust project
under their published terms. PostgreSQL claims cite the PostgreSQL documentation. Citation
identifies technical mechanics and does not incorporate the complete text of those works.

Academic references identify the established families this doctrine refines: typestate-oriented
programming, behavioral types, object protocols, and session types. They are cited for the
concepts they introduced. None of them defines the specific mechanism named in this package, and
this package does not claim their authority for it.

Tool behavior is cited with a version: `trybuild` 1.0.118, Rust 1.97.1 pinned and 1.85.0
minimum, checked 2026-08-04. Diagnostic rendering is a changing fact and is rechecked when the
pinned toolchain moves.

## Research limit

This package records the sources actually used. It does not claim exhaustive coverage of
typestate literature, session-type theory, protocol verification, workflow formalisms, or
database concurrency research. The absence of a source is not a judgment about it.

No quotation long enough to require separate license analysis is reproduced. No external media,
transcript, or specification text is mirrored. Repository licensing applies to the original
doctrine prose and examples here, and makes no claim over the cited works.
