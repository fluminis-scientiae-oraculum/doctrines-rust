# RUST-DOC-0009 attribution

Benchmark and profiler projects are cited for their own mechanisms. Results
remain tool-, environment-, workload-, and version-specific. The doctrine does
not reproduce manuals or claim one profiler is universally appropriate.

Workload contracts, claim scoping, correctness preservation, complexity
tradeoffs, guarantee ledgers, and review severity are repository synthesis.

This package is not an exhaustive performance-engineering bibliography. It does
not define statistical methods beyond requiring their disclosure and
appropriate interpretation. Maintainers must verify tool/platform behavior for
each executable benchmark environment.
